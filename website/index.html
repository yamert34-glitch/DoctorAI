01 THE PROJECT

When communication is limited,
preparation matters.

Long-distance missions can involve communication delays or periods without contact. Doctor AI demonstrates how an onboard tool can use a crew member’s questionnaire answers and medical references to suggest possible diagnoses and practical next steps while they wait to connect with mission control.

The prototype runs its language model locally, bringing the guided questionnaire, reference context, and generated assessment together in one working flow.

02 HOW IT WORKS

From symptoms to
an assessment.

The local LLM uses questionnaire answers, its system instructions, and medical-reference excerpts to generate possible explanations and steps to consider.

  1. 01

    Choose a profile

    Start a local profile or select an existing one. Face matching is an experimental profile-selection option.

  2. 02

    Answer guided questions

    Share symptoms, when they began, relevant history, medications, and other requested details.

  3. 03

    Generate an LLM assessment

    The local language model considers the answers alongside its system instructions and medical-reference excerpts, then suggests possible conditions and next steps.

  4. 04

    Review and follow protocol

    Review the AI-generated assessment as a possibility, not a certainty. Follow onboard medical protocol for care decisions.

FAST ESCALATION · PROTOTYPE FEATURE

Serious phrases can bypass model wait time.

Before a model assessment, a local phrase checker scans answers for configured high-risk terms and symptom phrases—for example, reports involving blood or severe chest and heart symptoms. When it finds a match, the prototype skips model generation and immediately displays a fixed urgent warning: the reported symptoms may indicate a very serious situation, and the user should follow the onboard medical protocol.

In current team testing, a model-generated assessment can take about 1 minute 40 seconds. For a matched phrase, the direct-message path is designed to respond in under 1–2 seconds instead of waiting for generation.

This phrase checker is an early safeguard, not a complete emergency detector: it may miss urgent wording or match a phrase without understanding context. No alert does not mean a situation is safe. Follow onboard protocol whenever symptoms are concerning.

AI-generated possibilities may be wrong or incomplete. Use the prototype's output as information for review, not as a confirmed diagnosis.

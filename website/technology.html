THE PHYSICAL PROTOTYPE

A build made for hands-on iteration.

The project has been assembled and tested on regular computers with a touchscreen setup. The board and sketches also record separate electronics and enclosure experiments.

Touchscreen + keyboardRead questions, enter answers, and review the generated assessment.
CameraUsed for experimental profile matching—not for medical image analysis.
ESP32-S3 sensor experimentReads a pulse sensor over serial when configured; a missing or unreliable reading is recorded as unknown.
Case and board studiesCAD and brainstorm images capture design directions, not proof of flight-ready hardware.

Built and tested on regular computers. Raspberry Pi/Linux is a target environment; current software has not been tested on spacecraft hardware. Radiation tolerance, multi-week autonomy, and spacecraft compatibility remain engineering challenges—not established capabilities.
